01 / The context

Overview

Janna’s Bowls is a fast-growing culinary brand based in Lagos, specializing in rich, authentic Nigerian dishes, smoky jollof rice, and food bowls. Formerly operating as 'Janna's Treats', the brand had outgrown its original identity and needed a modern, premium visual system capable of supporting future business expansion.

02 / The problem

The Challenge

  • Category Limitation: The name 'Treats' communicated confectionery and pastries, confusing customers looking for full meals and traditional soups.
  • Visual Clutter: The old emblem combined a gradient badge, fork illustration, and script font, making it illegible at smaller scales.
  • Scalability Bottleneck: The visual identity was rigid and could not stretch to accommodate new sub-brands like catering, food sauces, or a culinary academy.

03 / The thinking

The Strategy & Naming

We transitioned the brand from 'Janna’s Treats' to 'Janna’s Bowls' to immediately reflect its core offering while retaining brand equity. To prepare for long-term growth, I designed a wordmark-driven master brand. By choosing a custom, fluid script paired with clean sans-serif secondary typography, the brand achieves two things:

  • Approachability & Warmth: Reflecting home-cooked care and hospitality.
  • Scalability: A clean system that easily adapts across sub-brands (Janna's Catering, Janna's Kitchen, Janna's Academy) using unified badge lockups and a custom 'J's' monogram for small-space applications.
04 / The visual language

Color Psychology & Typography

  • Deep Burnt Orange Red: Triggers appetite, evoking the rich tones of smoky jollof rice and palm oil dishes.
  • Warm Cream: A clean, breathable base color that elevates the brand above low-cost fast-food aesthetic.
  • Dark Charcoal: Provides high-contrast grounding for packaging, signage, and digital communication.
