Brand Identity

Luminous Aesthetics

Client Luminous Inc.
Timeline 4 Weeks
Deliverables Logo, Web UI, Social Kit

The Challenge

Luminous Inc. was entering a saturated market with a revolutionary tech product, but their visual identity felt heavy and outdated. They needed a brand system that communicated clarity, speed, and cutting-edge innovation without relying on cliché tech tropes. They needed to feel like light.

The Solution

I stripped away all non-essential elements, creating a stark monochromatic palette relying purely on black, deep charcoal, and pure white. The brand was injected with subtle glow effects and typography that commands authority. The result is a confident, radiant presence that instantly positions them as a premium market leader.
