Brand Identity 2024 Luminary Co.

Building a Brand That Commands Every Room.

A complete visual identity system for Luminary Co. — from logo and type to colour, motion, and digital presence.

Client

Luminary Co.

Timeline

6 Weeks

Deliverables

Logo, Brand Guide, Social Kit, Website

The Problem

A great product with no visual voice.

Luminary Co. had a premium product and loyal customers — but their visual identity didn't match the quality they delivered. Inconsistent branding was eroding trust before customers even made contact.

They needed a complete identity rebuild. Something that felt elevated, confident, and ready to compete at the highest level.

What we needed to solve.

Create a cohesive visual identity that communicated premium quality at every touchpoint — from social media to packaging to web presence.

The new brand needed to feel timeless, modern, and instantly recognisable in a crowded market.

The Solution

A complete identity system built to last.

We built the brand from the ground up — starting with strategic brand positioning, then moving into visual expression. Every element was designed with intention: the logo mark, the typographic system, the colour palette, the motion language, and the digital design system.

The result is a brand that speaks before a word is said. Clean. Confident. Luminous.

Numbers that speak.

Within 60 days of the new brand launch, Luminary Co. saw measurable results across every metric that matters.
